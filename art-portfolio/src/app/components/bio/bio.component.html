Biography

Painter • Sculptor • Classical Guitarist • Software Engineer

Scott Weckbaugh

Born in Denver, Colorado, and currently based in Des Moines, Washington, Scott Weckbaugh operates at the intersection of classical craftsmanship and modern analytical discipline. As a painter, sculptor, software engineer, and classical guitarist, his work reflects a rigorous dedication to technique, structure, and emotional depth.

Scott’s fine art practice focuses on oil painting and sculpture, drawing deep inspiration from classical traditions. Whether rendering landscapes or figurative studies, his approach emphasizes atmospheric tone, dramatic light, and structural precision.
